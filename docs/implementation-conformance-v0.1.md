## Status key and conclusion

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Implemented** | The bounded behavior exists in this worktree and has direct focused test or artifact evidence. It is not automatically a released capability. |
| **Partial** | Useful behavior exists, but one or more master-specification requirements or evidence gates are absent. |
| **Not implemented** | No conforming implementation/evidence exists in this worktree. |

The current worktree is a **partial research kernel**, not a conforming/accepted
QCore v0.1 release. Its narrow routing-placement fixture recorded
`vertical-slice-go`; a later 15-pair exact-small development cohort recorded
**research continue**, including one material regression against the stronger
same-attempt Qiskit baseline. Both artifacts record the master-specification
v0.1 gate as `not_evaluated`. The full experiment has not run.

The most consequential open items are the complete external compiler portfolio,
fair resource-equalized and held-out evaluation, full tiered semantic
verification, locked replay from complete committed bundles, the complete
logical artifact set, planning budgets/worker isolation, and independent review.
The public/import name also remains `qplanck`, not the conceptual `qcore` package
shown in the master specification.

## Current decision and next acceptance step

The smoke evidence is promising enough to continue the controlled experiment,
but it cannot justify a product-level **Go**. The next acceptance step is to
freeze the supported semantic profile, recipes, targets, budgets, correctness
floor, and held-out corpus; then run the full master-specification experiment
against strong equal-budget baselines and have it independently reviewed.

Until then, allowed wording should remain narrow: QCore has an offline,
explainable planning research-kernel vertical slice, and one synthetic fixture
showed a correctness-preserving selection improvement under its declared proxy.
